      Questions

      How are reps counted?

      Each time a wrist reverses direction after travelling a set distance, it counts as one rep. The distance scales with your body size, so it does not depend on how far you stand.

      Is my video uploaded?

      No. Tracking runs in your browser. Only the pose model files are downloaded.

      Why is my count lower than expected?

      Most webcams capture about 30 frames per second, so very fast pumping can be undercounted. Good light and both wrists in frame help.

      Does it work on a phone?

      It should wherever your browser allows camera access. A laptop webcam is the safest choice for frame rate.

      Is there a leaderboard?

      Not yet. Your best score is saved on this device.
